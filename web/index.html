02 / Evaluation protocol

One model. One declared run.

Public white-box suite
01

Give it the open tasks.

Use a fixed public snapshot of the eligible inclusion questions, definitions and known implications. The model may inspect the suite; this is not a hidden test set.

02

Declare the conditions.

Record the model version, tools and resource budget before collecting answers. Preserve the prompts and outputs as one identified evaluation run.

03

Verify its proofs.

Require a proof, disproof or independence result for each claimed resolution. Mathematical review and the required formal checks decide what is accepted.

04

Count all consequences.

Combine the run's accepted proofs with reviewed implications. Award one point per distinct eligible pair, whether resolved directly or as a consequence.

05 / Scope & provenance

What the score measures.

The benchmark measures an AI run's ability to resolve open questions about total-language complexity classes. It is intended for mathematical reasoning beyond the current public baseline. It does not attempt to cover every kind of progress in complexity theory: promise-only separations, individual algorithmic speedups, search problems and fixed-exponent lower bounds can fall outside this suite.
