04 / Scope & provenance

A precise score needs a precise scope.

01

Resolve the statement.

A point concerns one ordered inclusion. A proof, a disproof, or an accepted independence result can settle it. Repeated derivations earn no extra points.

02

Count its consequences.

Inclusions, noninclusions, complement identities and reviewed conditional theorems propagate through the graph. Each result retains a trace to its premises and sources.

03

Keep the limits visible.

These are total-language classes. Promise-only separations, many algorithmic speedups, search problems and fixed-exponent lower bounds need other measures.
