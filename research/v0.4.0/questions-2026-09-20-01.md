# Roster review questions — 2026-09-20, pass 01

## 1. Representation scope

Pending: Should v0.4.0 be allowed to add separately typed promise, search/function or algebraic tracks if the evidence shows they are needed to represent important questions, or should this revision stay within one matrix of total decision-language classes?

Why this matters: many canonical quantum questions concern promise problems; #P, PPAD and VP/VNP do not have the same objects as total decision-language classes. Mixing their labels in an untyped inclusion matrix would be mathematically misleading.

While awaiting an answer, research both possibilities and keep the current decision-language matrix as the implementation baseline. Do not change the scored universe merely because a broader track is easier to populate. The original approximately 40–50-class target, historical cutoff and one-point-per-open-pair rule remain working assumptions.

## Answer received

Thomas: “Keep this revision to total decision-language classes.”

Accepted decision: v0.4.0 retains the existing total binary decision-language universe. No promise, search/function or algebraic track is added. The current eleven-swap proposal already follows this scope. Broader tracks remain possible future work, with separately defined objects and scoring rules.
